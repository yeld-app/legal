2. Functionality

The App lets you review, keep, and delete photos and videos stored on your device. It can also make smaller copies of your videos, turn Live Photos into still photos, convert ProRAW photos to HEIF, read the text in your screenshots so you can search and save it as notes, and group your photos into trips and events.

3. Responsibility

You are responsible for reviewing photos and videos before deletion. Items you delete in the App first go to Yeld Trash and are removed from your library only when you empty Yeld Trash and confirm the iOS prompt. After that, they may be recoverable only according to iOS/Photos behavior (for example, Recently Deleted), and may become permanently unrecoverable after final removal.

Compressed and converted copies. Compressing a video, turning a Live Photo into a photo, or converting a ProRAW photo creates a new copy with less data than the original (lower bitrate or resolution, no motion, or no RAW data). The original is moved to Yeld Trash. Once you empty Yeld Trash, the original quality cannot be restored from the copy. If you want to keep the full-quality original elsewhere, save it first (for example, with “Save the original first”).

Automatic titles. Titles and topics for screenshots on devices with Apple Intelligence are generated automatically and may be inaccurate.
